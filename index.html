FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Write to our team.

When can I get one?

We make kits in small batches and work through the list in order. We'll write to you personally before we make yours.

Does joining cost anything?

No. When their box is ready we send the total and a secure payment link. If the timing no longer works, just say no thanks.

What if we need it by a certain date?

Pick "By a date" when you join the list and tell us the date. If we can make it in time we will, and if we can't, we'll tell you honestly.

Are these medical devices?

No. They're everyday aids that make ordinary tasks easier. If their care team has recommended specific equipment, follow that first.
