FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Email our team.

When can I get one?

We make kits in small batches and work through the list in order. Join for the kit you need, and we'll be in touch personally before we make yours.

Does joining cost anything?

No. When their box is ready we send the total and a secure payment link. If the timing no longer works, just say no thanks.

What if we need it by a certain date?

Email us with the date. If we can make it in time we will — and if we can't, we'll tell you honestly.

What if something isn't right for them?

The No-Drawer Guarantee: we replace or refund any item that doesn't fit their life — 60 days, and printed pieces don't need to be shipped back.

Are these medical devices?

No. They're everyday aids that make ordinary tasks easier. If their care team has recommended specific equipment, follow that advice first.
