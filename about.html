Our story

We outfitted one house for recovery. Then we boxed it.

In 2026, our founder's grandmother came home from a stroke to a house that suddenly didn't fit her — jars, buttons and playing cards all needed two hands.

So we did what makers do: printed, tested and printed again, until the card holder, key turner and plate guard worked for her. Where a store-bought tool was better, we bought it.

The people who buy get-well gifts stand in an aisle of flowers and balloons, while the person they love is at home fighting a jar.

Bounce Back Kits is the box we wish someone had handed us that first week — made a few at a time, so the first families can help us get every kit right.

What we promise

Six promises, in plain language

  • Honest about what these are

    Everyday aids — not medical devices, not miracle gadgets.

  • Curated, not cloned

    Printed where ours is better; store-bought where it isn't.
