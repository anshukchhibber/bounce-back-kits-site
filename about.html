Our story

We outfitted one house for recovery. Then we boxed it.

When our founder's grandmother came home after a stroke, the house suddenly didn't fit her. Jars, buttons and playing cards all needed two hands.

So we did what makers do. We printed, tested and printed again, until the card holder, the key turner and the plate guard worked for her. Where a store-bought tool was better, we bought it.

Bounce Back Kits is the box we wish someone had handed us that first week. We make a few at a time, so the first families can help us get every kit right.
